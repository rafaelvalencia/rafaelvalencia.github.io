I work at [TRATON R&D](https://traton.com/en.html) ([Scania](https://www.scania.com/)), where I develop software for autonomous driving and contribute to industrial research and advanced engineering projects. My work focuses on enabling autonomous systems to estimate their own state, understand their surroundings, and make reliable decisions.

Before joining TRATON, I worked on autonomous driving at [Zenseact](https://zenseact.com/) (formerly Zenuity). I also held research positions at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/)’s [Robotics Institute](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/) in the USA and at [Halmstad University](https://www.hh.se/english.html) and [Örebro University](https://www.oru.se/english/) in Sweden. I earned my PhD from [Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC)](https://www.upc.edu/en), Spain.

My research has spanned autonomous mobile robot navigation and autonomous driving, including simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM), state estimation, road understanding, and motion planning.
